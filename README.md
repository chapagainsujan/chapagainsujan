## About Me

I am an **agriculture researcher** working in **crop protection, plant breeding and horticulture**. My work combines field and laboratory research with statistics and programming, so that biological and agricultural data can be analysed correctly and reproducibly.

I build open-source tools in R for researchers who deal with non-normal, skewed or messy data, such as soil pH, yield, disease scores and concentrations. Find my publications and research profiles under [**Workspaces**](#workspaces) below.

## R Packages (Developed / Maintained)

- **[transform](https://github.com/chapagainsujan/transform)**  
  Tests your data for normality and, if needed, compares log, square-root, Box-Cox, Yeo-Johnson and rank-based inverse normal transformations, picks the best one, and can reverse it back to the original scale. Built for agricultural and biological data.
